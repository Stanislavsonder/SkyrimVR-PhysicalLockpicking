$VRLP_HeaderModelMarkup	Model markup
$VRLP_MarkupMode	Model markup tool
$VRLP_MarkupMode_Help	While lockpicking: 0) with the lock on the door or chest, place it there: hold the left trigger to carry it with your hand; 1) put the pick's tip on the keyhole's centre, then its top, with the left trigger - the lock model moves to fit; 2) and 3) line up the pick and the tension wrench in your hands: hold the left trigger to keep the model still while you move your hand. A / X goes to the next part. The number pad fine-tunes each part. Saved per model.

$VRLP_ResetMarkup_Help	Forgets every lock keyhole and tool grip you saved with the model markup tool: the built-in alignments and the automatic fit are used again.
